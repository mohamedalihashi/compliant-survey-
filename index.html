Title: Mobile Service Experience Survey
Purpose of this research
This survey is part of a thesis study at Jamhuriya University. The goal is to understand common problems users experience with mobile operators – such as network, internet, mobile money, billing, or SIM card issues – and to train a computer system to automatically recognise and classify these complaints from text..

Hoos ka dooro shirkadda telecom-ka ee la xiriirta cabasho kasta.

Required

1. Network / Shabakad

Cabasho ku saabsan signal la'aan, wicitaan go'a, coverage liidata, ama SMS dhibaato leh.

Required

2. Internet

Cabasho ku saabsan internet gaabis ah, data aan shaqayn, bundle dhibaato leh, ama 3G/4G/5G cilad.

Required

3. Mobile Money

Cabasho ku saabsan lacag dirid, lacag helid, transaction fashilmay, ama mobile wallet cilad.

Required

4. Billing / Lacag jarid

Cabasho ku saabsan lacag si khaldan loo jaray, balance lumay, fee aan la fahmin, ama xirmo aan furmin.

Required

5. SIM Card Issue

Cabasho ku saabsan SIM xiran, SIM aan shaqayn, diiwaangelin, replacement, activation, PIN ama PUK.
